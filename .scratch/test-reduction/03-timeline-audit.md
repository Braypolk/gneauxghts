## Test-only cleanup and implementation limits

Deleting1–2 leaves an empty `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` in editing_window_policy; delete it. Deleting6 likely makes ObservationReceipt test-only `source`, `path`, `previous_path`, `observed_at_millis`, `modified_at_millis` accessors unused (25 physical lines). Keep `kind`: identity/reattachment tests still call it. Deleting5 likely leaves its test-only payload-version/source accessors unused; remove only after reference/compiler checks. Production fields, types, policies and methods stay untouched. Remove unused imports arising solely from these deletions; do not opportunistically reformat shared files.

Expected guaranteed savings:13 executable cases and384 test lines. Additional test-only helper savings are separately measured after compilation; do not count them as production simplification. Setup removed includes six full vault/AppState fixtures (some with multiple saves), notably the80-save duplicate plus the fabricated task delivery race. No timing claim is made without measurement.

## Rejected unsafe cuts and retained modules

- **All receipt/durable transaction boundaries remain:** exact scope, predecessor/identity mismatch, prepared-baseline verification, receipt-outcome and preparation-removal rollback, sealing rollback, orphan preparation/missing receipt, corrupted pending payload/anchor/time evidence. Similar names do not mean equivalent failure locations.
- **Keep all target-readiness/race cases:** queue admission versus canonical read, writer/recovery, close barrier, invalidated verification success and failure, concurrent background diagnostic replacement, queued observation replay, authoritative task transform, rename rollback and indeterminate rollback. These distinguish different lock/lifetime owners.
- **Keep thresholds and boundedness:**128 replay/checkpoint chain boundary,64 receipt boundary and protected live receipts, history-page30 boundary, candidate/read/search capacity boundaries. Retain `settled_publications_do_not_retain_full_authored_copies`: its allocated/live-storage assertion after large identical and abandoned saves is not equivalent to counting prepared rows.
- **Keep provenance cases:** full body/properties, creation source, duplicate movement, formatting context, adjacent edits, unterminated append, exact selected restore lineage after fault/restart, intervals versus points, dropped intermediate prose, scope exclusions and stale citations. No test slated for deletion is their only oracle.
- **Evidence:** no whole-test deletions recommended. The small `budgets_preserve_utf8_and_rank_fusion_rewards_agreement` looks shallow, but its invalid numeric after/before check is the only direct assertion of that legacy request branch; deleting the whole test would silently remove it. Keep operation snapshot/cache invalidation, response-overhead budget, mixed valid/failed pages, UTF-8 tail, literal/regex exactness, timezone/DST/half-open bounds, dense deadline omission, activity/current-status distinction and assistant transcript exclusion. Their current production paths differ.
- **Task dates:** retain all nine tests, including portable frontend fixture, protected Markdown, CRLF/UTF-8/hardbreak mutation and clipped/bounded passage metadata. **Note catalog:** retain all five tests; managed-chat projection separation, duplicate identity, generation ordering, pending retry and settled-watermark iteration are separate production obligations. **Current document:** no local executable case to remove.
- **History command fixtures and errors:** keep serialized cross-language fixtures/redaction, real corruption latches, runnable cheap IPC while publication blocks and canonical bootstrap without history admission. Shallow constructed mapping table alone is redundant.
- **Forgotten commands:** preserve distinct ordinary-note/chat archive, stale selected purge, destination collision, partial write rollback, concurrent metadata/navigation, unreadable identity, retention/restart and generated unmanaged identity contracts.
